# Human Taste Network

Status: A20AT export/import-ready layer.

## Purpose

The Human Taste Network prepares NeuroChess visual variants for human feedback without pretending that AI or local scoring equals real human taste. It supports provider-ready packets, local/manual vote import, conservative confidence scoring, and continued autonomous work when no human data exists.

## Validation Levels

LEVEL 0 - Internal / Local Provisional:
Generated by local scoring, Mission Doctor, NeuroRelay, and design heuristics. It can guide the next mission, but cannot claim human approval.

LEVEL 1 - Owner Taste:
User or owner vote. It can select a product-direction preference, but cannot claim general crowd majority.

LEVEL 2 - Crowd Human Validation:
Real external human responses from a panel or research tool. Only this level can support majority validation claims.

## Supported Providers

- PickFu-style packets: quick preference and art-direction checks.
- Useberry-style packets: five-second, preference, first-click, and clarity checks.
- Lyssna-style packets: survey and usability checks with recruited participants.
- Maze-style packets: prototype/usability and task-success checks.
- Local/manual packets: owner vote or small anonymous CSV/JSON import.

All paid providers are export-only by default. A20AT does not submit paid studies, spend money, or call provider APIs.

## Evidence Source

The network consumes A20AS isolated variant screenshots for:

- sacred_board_chamber A/B/C
- decision_feedback_language A/B/C

Contact sheets are navigation aids only. Isolated full and main-surface screenshots are the primary evidence references.

## Allowed Claims

- HUMAN_DATA_ABSENT
- PROVISIONAL_LOCAL_WINNER
- OWNER_TASTE_SELECTED
- CROWD_TEST_READY
- CROWD_RESULTS_IMPORTED
- MAJORITY_PREFERENCE_SUPPORTED
- WEIRDNESS_RISK_LOW
- HUMAN_VALIDATION_INCONCLUSIVE
- HUMAN_VALIDATION_FAILED

## Forbidden Claims

- A universal 95 percent appeal claim.
- "Crowd validated" without level 2 crowd data.
- "Owner vote equals market majority."
- "Synthetic panel equals human data."

## Automation Behavior

If no human results are present, the autonomous loop continues with provisional local scoring. Missing human data is a state, not a failure.
