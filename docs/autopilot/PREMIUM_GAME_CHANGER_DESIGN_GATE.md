# Premium Game-Changer Design Gate

NeuroChess should not settle for a merely competent chess UI. The game-changer
gate asks whether a design feels like the future NeuroChess Decision Arena, not
just a clean prototype.

Pass signals:

- the board reads as the central artifact;
- the first viewport has a strong sense of place;
- state changes are visible without relying on inspector text;
- atmosphere supports the chess decision rather than competing with it;
- feedback is energetic, honest, and not gamey;
- visual depth feels premium rather than noisy;
- the design would be memorable after one glance.

Warn or fail signals:

- the scene feels like a CSS demo;
- labels explain what the visuals fail to communicate;
- the board is technically centered but not visually authoritative;
- HUD boxes dominate the stage;
- glow, traces, or 3D effects feel decorative;
- chess readability is traded for spectacle.

`GAME_CHANGER_PASS` is not required for every prototype, but it is required
before declaring a winner as the long-term visual direction.
