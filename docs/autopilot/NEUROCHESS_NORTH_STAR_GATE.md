# NeuroChess North Star Gate

## North Star

NeuroChess must become an addictive, game-like, truth-driven chess learning
system that helps any player unlock their real chess potential as fast as
possible through:

- real-game decisions;
- active replay;
- honest feedback;
- repetition;
- transfer verification;
- adaptive planning.

## Potential Unlock Loop

Every product mission should identify which part of this loop it advances:

1. Detect a meaningful decision in a real game.
2. Isolate that decision into a playable learning moment.
3. Let the player try before seeing the answer.
4. Judge the decision truthfully.
5. Explain why the position changed.
6. Replay until stable.
7. Re-expose the moment later.
8. Verify transfer in future real games.
9. Adapt the plan based on transfer evidence.

## Why The Gate Exists

Automation can become impressive while the product stands still. The North Star
Gate forces future missions to state the player friction they reduce and the
learning-loop value they create.

## Science-Safe Rules

The gate rejects fake progress:

- no fake neuroscience claims;
- no brain, cortex, or synapse marketing;
- no fake Elo prediction;
- no false XP, rank, or Transfer claims;
- no claim of progress without evidence.

Safety and automation missions can still pass when they clearly unlock a
specific product mission.
