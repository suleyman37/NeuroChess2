# NeuroChess Piece Identity Brief

NeuroChess pieces must be immediately readable and visually premium without
becoming external asset dependencies.

## Requirements

- immediately readable;
- premium;
- not letter discs for North Star screens;
- not overly decorative;
- no external assets by default;
- works at small sizes;
- compatible with board clarity;
- distinct enough to feel NeuroChess over time.

## Allowed Directions

- styled Unicode pieces;
- internal SVG silhouettes;
- code-native vector pieces;
- existing internal project chess components if available;
- high-contrast piece treatment for dark and light squares.

## Forbidden Directions

- downloaded piece assets without a dedicated asset mission;
- ornamental pieces that hurt recognition;
- pieces that only work at large size;
- letter-token placeholders for public-ready claims.

## Acceptance

Piece identity can be distinctive only after it remains readable across 1366,
1440, and 1920 screenshots.
