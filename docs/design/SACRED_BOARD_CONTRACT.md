# Sacred Board Contract

The chessboard is the protected learning surface. NeuroChess may build a rich
world around the board, but the board itself stays strict, readable, and
pedagogically honest.

Core rule:

> Strict board, rich world. The chessboard is sacred. The stage is expressive.

## Board Core

The board core has maximum restriction:

- true 8x8 grid;
- square dimensions appear uniform;
- top-down or near top-down;
- no perspective that harms chess reading;
- pieces immediately readable;
- no fog, particles, decorative rails, or atmosphere inside the board surface;
- no line, arrow, path, destination glow, or candidate hint before feedback;
- no visual language that implies a best move before the player has tried.

Any failure blocks product-grade readiness.

## Board Frame

The frame may express state, but it must not become a hint.

Allowed:

- premium depth;
- rim light;
- soft state energy;
- non-directional tension;
- subtle material response.

Forbidden:

- arrow-like effects before feedback;
- destination glow before feedback;
- line effects crossing the board before feedback;
- frame effects that make the board hard to read.

## Surrounding Stage

The surrounding stage has the most freedom. It may carry NeuroChess identity:

- living chamber;
- tactical architecture;
- memory rails outside the board;
- state-based lighting;
- atmosphere and material depth;
- cockpit navigation.

The stage must never take authority away from the board.

## Post-Feedback Pedagogy

After feedback, the board may show teaching information:

- move trace;
- correction trace;
- replay path;
- consequence cue.

The visual language must be elegant, inspectable, and non-humiliating. A miss is
reorientation, not punishment.

## Acceptance

A board-stage surface cannot be product-grade unless:

- chessboard fidelity passes;
- anti-spoiler semantics pass;
- board pieces are readable;
- pre-feedback board surface is clean;
- post-feedback traces are clearly post-feedback;
- no fake XP, rank, Elo, Transfer, neuroscience, or Practice-ready claim exists.
