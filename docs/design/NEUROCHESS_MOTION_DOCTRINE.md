# NeuroChess Motion Doctrine

Motion exists to communicate learning state. It is never free decoration.

## State Motion

- observe: calm, readable, stable.
- try_before_feedback: tension outside board, no answer.
- feedback_success: resolution, confirmation, insight stabilizing.
- feedback_miss: reorientation, correction without humiliation.
- replay: memory, reconstruction, controlled path.

## Rules

- no motion that delays training;
- no random particles on the board;
- no camera spin;
- no pre-feedback destination motion;
- reduced motion fallback is required;
- motion must be interruptible and state-bound.

## Durations

- micro response: 120-180 ms;
- feedback pulse: 300-600 ms;
- replay path: controlled and skippable;
- atmosphere: subtle and non-critical.
