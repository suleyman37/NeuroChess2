# Awwwards-Grade Chess App Benchmark

Core phrase:

> Awwwards-grade craft, application-grade usability, chess-grade clarity.

Awwwards-grade does not mean NeuroChess becomes a website. It means high craft
inside a desktop chess learning application.

## Benchmark Dimensions

Score each dimension from 0 to 5.

1. Composition and first viewport.
2. Board-as-artifact treatment.
3. Visual hierarchy.
4. Typography and density.
5. Material and depth.
6. Motion and transitions.
7. Interaction feedback.
8. Scene identity.
9. Emotional pull.
10. Product usability.
11. Accessibility and performance discipline.
12. Chess clarity.

## Total Bands

- 0-20: weak prototype.
- 21-35: decent app UI.
- 36-45: premium direction.
- 46-55: Awwwards-inspired app craft.
- 56-60: signature NeuroChess screen.

## Translation Rules

Use Awwwards principles as craft translation:

- composition becomes first-viewport command;
- motion becomes state meaning;
- material becomes board-as-artifact depth;
- interaction becomes tactile learning feedback;
- memorability becomes NeuroChess identity.

## Forbidden Misreadings

- landing page instead of app;
- scroll storytelling;
- decorative 3D;
- hidden chess clarity;
- animations that delay training;
- board pollution;
- spectacle over learning;
- generic award-site aesthetics pasted onto chess.

## Pass Condition

A visual mission cannot claim Awwwards-inspired app craft unless chess clarity
and product usability both pass.
