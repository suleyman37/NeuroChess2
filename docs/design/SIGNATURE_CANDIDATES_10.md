# Signature Candidates 10

These candidates are DEV-only visual probes. They do not change V1 product behavior and do not claim user-facing truth until selected and integrated through a later mission.

## 1. sacred_board_chamber

- Description: A strict central board inside a quiet chamber frame.
- Learning-loop stage improved: Review focus.
- Factual contribution: Separates legal-square reading from surrounding atmosphere.
- Testable proof: Board squares remain rectangular, readable, and unobstructed in screenshots.
- Decorative risk: 1/3.
- Board readability risk: 0/3.
- Technical feasibility: 3/3.
- Anti-pattern: Distorted board, glowing squares, or atmosphere covering legal squares.
- Probe route target: `/app?visualProbeGallery=1`.
