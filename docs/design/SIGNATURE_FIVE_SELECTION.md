## Why Each Selected Component Was Selected

`sacred_board_chamber` is the strongest core identity. It makes the board feel protected and intentional without compromising square geometry.

`decision_feedback_language` is the strongest direct learning component. It turns the Try -> Feedback moment into a visible system while preserving the no-spoiler rule.

`critical_moment_sigil` gives moment selection a memorable mark without exposing raw engine or criticality internals.

`decision_pressure_field` covers the Attention/Decision moment. It makes decision pressure perceptible while keeping evaluation signals outside the board.

`memory_cabinet` anchors revision and return. Its current visual is less distinctive than the top four, so it should be strengthened through variants before integration.

## Rejected Candidates

`aftermath_timeline` is a strong reserve, but its current image is less distinctive than the selected components and overlaps reflection/revision.

`position_resonance` is promising, but transfer language has calibration risk and the two-board comparison adds complexity before transfer evidence exists.

`verdict_wax_seal` is memorable, but it overlaps with feedback language and has badge/reward-currency risk.

`piece_identity_system` is foundational later, but current evidence proves isolated silhouettes rather than board-scale piece readability.

`piece_breath` has the highest motion/decorative risk and the weakest direct product utility.

## Redundancy Considerations

The selected five avoid choosing both feedback verdict marks, avoid overloading the set with transfer/replay widgets, and preserve one component each for board identity, feedback, moment marking, attempt pressure, and revision memory.
