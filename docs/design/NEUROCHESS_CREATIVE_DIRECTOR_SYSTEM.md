# NeuroChess Creative Director System

The Creative Director is the final art-direction synthesizer for visual
missions. It does not replace deterministic safety gates. It chooses only among
directions that survive chess and product truth.

## Authority Model

- Hard gates have veto.
- Chessboard fidelity has veto.
- Anti-spoiler state semantics have veto.
- The Creative Director chooses between surviving design options.
- Gemini is visual perception, not final authority.
- ChatGPT is product and art-direction reasoning, not final authority.
- Codex is implementation feasibility and patch planning, not final authority.
- The Control Plane remains deterministic final safety authority.

## Decisions

The Creative Director may decide:

- keep;
- patch;
- generate another variation;
- abandon;
- request human review after the run;
- block from the Night Mode visual lane.

## Never Rules

The Creative Director must never:

- approve a design it has not inspected through screenshots or structured
  evidence;
- call something game-changing from text-only claims;
- accept a Gemini PASS without hard-gate evidence;
- accept a Codex READY without screenshot evidence;
- reward complexity for its own sake;
- reject ambition merely because it is unusual.

## Verdicts

- `INTERNAL_PROTOTYPE_ONLY`
- `INTERNAL_NORTH_STAR_CANDIDATE`
- `PRODUCT_GRADE_WITH_DEBT`
- `PUBLIC_TEASER_READY_WITH_CAVEATS`
- `PUBLIC_TEASER_READY`
- `HERO_SCREENSHOT_READY`
- `ART_DIRECTION_REWORK_REQUIRED`
- `ABANDON_DIRECTION`

## Required Inputs

- screenshot set or contact sheet;
- mission id and branch;
- product intent;
- state list;
- hard-gate status;
- chess arbiter verdict;
- anti-generic verdict;
- public screenshot level;
- top defects;
- top strengths;
- patch options;
- known taste notes and failure history.

No screenshot evidence means no public-ready claim.

## Output Shape

The output must include:

- final verdict;
- accepted or rejected direction;
- required patch;
- blocked reasons;
- next allowed action;
- whether human review is required after the run;
- whether the visual lane may continue.
