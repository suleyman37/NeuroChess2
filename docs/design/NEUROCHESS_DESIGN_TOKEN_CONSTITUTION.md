# NeuroChess Design Token Constitution

Future visual missions must use semantic tokens rather than random colors or
effects.

## Semantic Colors

- observe: calm teal, readable, low urgency.
- try_before_feedback: amber tension outside board.
- feedback_success: green insight, post-effort only.
- feedback_miss: warm correction, never punitive.
- replay: violet memory, explicitly post-feedback.
- danger: reserved for safety or hard block, not normal miss feedback.

## Typography

- desktop-first density;
- headings support state, not marketing fluff;
- no oversized landing-page hero type inside app surfaces;
- tabular numbers where measured values appear;
- readable labels at 1366 width.

## Material And Depth

- depth supports hierarchy and board-as-artifact;
- board core remains flat, clean, and readable;
- surrounding stage may be richer than the board.

## Glow Limits

- every glow follows the No Generic Glow Law;
- no glow token is added without state and function.

## Public Screenshot Requirements

- no debug labels;
- no DEV-only dominance;
- no unsupported product claim;
- no fake progress;
- board visible and readable.

No random visual tokens are allowed in future missions.
